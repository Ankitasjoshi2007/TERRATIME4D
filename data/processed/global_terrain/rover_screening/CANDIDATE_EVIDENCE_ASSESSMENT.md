## Limitations

- Copernicus GLO-90 is a digital surface model.
- Vegetation, buildings, ice and data quality can affect measurements.
- Geographic grouping does not establish accessibility or climate suitability.
- Candidates require higher-resolution imagery and mission-specific assessment.
