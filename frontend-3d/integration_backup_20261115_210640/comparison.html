11. Scientific Method and Data Information

Earth reference records come from the processed global terrain summary. Moon and Mars candidates come from the locally stored planetary grid. The algorithm ranks locations using terrain feature similarity, including slope, roughness and elevation when available. Similarity is a terrain analogy score, not proof of environmental, engineering or experimental safety. All comparisons use the available static local datasets.
