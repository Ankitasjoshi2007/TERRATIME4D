8. Scientific Notes

Analogue ranking uses terrain slope where comparable grid values are available. Earth elevation and planetary elevation are not treated as directly equivalent because planetary vertical datums differ. Earth 630 m roughness and planetary approximately 11 km roughness are shown separately and are not treated as measurements at the same scale.
